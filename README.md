### 🎮 **How to Play**

It's simple! Eat all the dots, avoid the ghosts, and aim for the High Score.

* **Move Up:** `[ ↑ ]` or `[ W ]`
* **Move Down:** `[ ↓ ]` or `[ S ]`
* **Move Left:** `[ ← ]` or `[ A ]`
* **Move Right:** `[ → ]` or `[ D ]`

### ✨ **Features**

* 🕹️ **Retro Graphics:** Classic 8-bit style.
* 🔊 **Sound Effects:** Original *Waka Waka* sounds.
* 🍒 **Bonus Fruit:** Eat the cherry for extra points!
* 🏆 **High Score:** Saves your best score automatically.
* 👻 **Power Pellets:** Turn the tables and eat the ghosts!
